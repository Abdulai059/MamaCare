## MamaLink Predictive Care Engine

It has **three responsibilities**:

### 1. Timeline Engine

Creates the expected care journey for every mother and child based on gestational age, delivery date, and child age.

### 2. Care Coordination Engine

Continuously compares **expected milestones** with **actual completed events** to identify missed ANC visits, overdue immunizations, delayed referrals, and other gaps in care.

### 3. Clinical Intelligence Engine

When a visit or assessment is completed, combines **clinical rules** with an LLM to generate explainable recommendations and caregiver education.
