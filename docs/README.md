
# MamaLink Project Overview

## Project Title

**MamaLink: AI-Powered Care Coordination Platform for Community Maternal and Newborn Health**

---

# Problem Statement

Across many communities in Ghana, Community-based Health Planning and Services (CHPS) workers are responsible for monitoring pregnant women and newborns. However, the current care process is largely reactive and fragmented.

Health workers often rely on paper records or basic digital systems that only capture information during visits. These systems do not actively identify women who have missed appointments, are at risk of complications, or require urgent follow-up. As a result, many mothers and newborns are lost to follow-up, referrals are not completed, and preventable complications are detected too late.

The problem is made worse by several realities:

* Limited healthcare workers serving large communities.
* Poor internet connectivity in many rural areas.
* Language barriers between health workers and caregivers.
* Lack of intelligent tools to prioritize high-risk cases.
* No automated care journey that tracks mothers from pregnancy through the early newborn period.

Current systems **record information**.

They do not **coordinate care**.

---

# Proposed Solution

MamaLink is an AI-powered Care Coordination Platform that helps CHPS workers proactively manage maternal and newborn care.

Instead of waiting for patients to return to the health facility, MamaLink automatically creates a personalized care journey when a pregnancy is registered. It continuously compares expected care milestones with completed activities to detect missed visits, overdue follow-ups, and clinical risks.

Using a hybrid Clinical Rules Engine and Large Language Model (ChatGPT or Claude), the platform generates explainable recommendations for health workers. Khaya AI then translates these recommendations into local Ghanaian languages and converts them into speech, enabling caregivers to receive understandable guidance regardless of literacy level.

The platform supports CHPS workers by identifying who needs attention first, while ensuring that clinical decisions remain under the responsibility of healthcare professionals.

---

# Core Innovation

MamaLink is **not** another electronic medical record.

It is a **Care Coordination Engine**.

It automatically:

* Generates care journeys.
* Detects overdue milestones.
* Prioritizes high-risk mothers and newborns.
* Provides explainable AI recommendations.
* Delivers local-language voice guidance.
* Supports offline-first workflows for rural communities.

# Scope of the MVP (3 Weeks)

The MVP focuses on one complete care journey:

**Pregnancy → Delivery → Early Newborn Care (0–6 weeks)**

Included features:

* Secure authentication.
* Supervisor-managed CHPS worker accounts.
* Region → District → Community → CHPS Compound hierarchy.
* Household registration.
* Pregnant woman registration.
* Automatic pregnancy care journey generation.
* Clinical assessment recording.
* Risk detection using Clinical Rules.
* AI-generated recommendations (ChatGPT/Claude).
* Local-language voice guidance using Khaya AI.
* Daily priority dashboard for CHPS workers.

---

# What Makes MamaLink Different?

Traditional systems ask:

> **"What information do you want to record?"**

MamaLink asks:

> **"Who needs care today?"**

Instead of acting as a digital register, MamaLink continuously monitors active care journeys, detects missed milestones, and recommends proactive interventions before complications become emergencies.

This transforms CHPS workers from reactive record keepers into proactive care coordinators.

---

# Expected Impact

By combining proactive care coordination, explainable AI, and local-language communication, MamaLink aims to:

* Improve attendance at antenatal and postnatal visits.
* Reduce loss to follow-up.
* Help CHPS workers prioritize high-risk cases.
* Improve caregiver understanding through voice guidance in local languages.
* Strengthen continuity of maternal and newborn care in low-resource settings.

---

# The Vision

> **MamaLink empowers CHPS workers with AI-driven care coordination to ensure that no pregnant woman or newborn is left behind.**

## One thing I would change from our earlier discussions

Bro, I think we should stop describing MamaLink as an **AI application**.

Instead, every document should consistently describe it as:

> **"An AI-powered Care Coordination Platform for CHPS workers."**

That subtle change matters. It tells the judges that AI is **a capability**, not the product itself. The product is **care coordination**, and AI, clinical rules, and Khaya AI are the technologies that make that coordination smarter, more proactive, and more accessible. I think that's a much stronger positioning for both the hackathon and future development.
